YOUR DATA.
YOUR CHOICE.

STMEM offers optional visitor analytics. Analytics are not enabled unless you choose “ALLOW ANALYTICS”.

What may be collected

  • IP address received by the hosting server
  • Browser user-agent and basic browser/device category
  • Operating-system/platform information supplied by your browser
  • Screen dimensions, language and visit timestamp

Why and who can see it

The information is used for basic site security and traffic understanding. Only the STMEM administrator account configured for this site can view visitor records. It is not displayed publicly or sold.

Your choice and retention

You can decline analytics and still use the sign-in page. Clearing this site's browser storage allows you to choose again. Revoking consent does not automatically erase records already collected; contact the site operator to request access or deletion. The operator should configure a short retention period, such as 30 days.

Login information

Google sign-in is handled by Google through Firebase Authentication. Email/password authentication is handled by Firebase Authentication. STMEM does not ask for or store your Google password.

Before publishing, add your operator contact details, actual retention period, and any disclosures required by applicable law.
